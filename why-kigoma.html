Working Where Development Matters

Kigoma is predominantly rural, with many communities depending on agriculture and natural resources for their livelihoods.

Communities face interconnected challenges involving health, education, environmental sustainability, agricultural productivity and limited economic opportunities.

TARUDEREO works with communities to develop practical and sustainable responses to these challenges.
