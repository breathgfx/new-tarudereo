Building Sustainable, Healthy and Resilient Rural Communities

Tanzania Rural Development and Relief Organization (TARUDEREO) is a registered non-governmental organization based in Kasulu, Kigoma Region, Tanzania, working with rural and underserved communities to promote sustainable development, improved health, education, environmental protection and economic opportunities.

Who We Are

TARUDEREO works alongside rural communities and local stakeholders to address development challenges and create sustainable opportunities for vulnerable and underserved populations. Our approach is based on community participation, local knowledge, partnerships, accountability and sustainable solutions.
