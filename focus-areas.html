Focus Areas

Our Focus Areas

🌱 Environment

We promote environmental protection, climate resilience, sustainable natural resource management, restoration and community awareness for a healthier and more sustainable environment.

❤️ Health

We support community-based approaches that contribute to improved health outcomes, health awareness, maternal and child wellbeing, nutrition, sanitation and access to essential health information.

🌾 Agriculture

We promote sustainable and climate-smart agriculture, improved food security, agricultural knowledge, livelihood diversification, value addition and better economic opportunities for rural households.

📚 Education

We support initiatives that improve access to education, learning opportunities, skills development and educational opportunities for vulnerable children, young people and communities.

💰 Micro-Economic Development

We promote household and community economic empowerment through entrepreneurship, financial literacy, small enterprise development, livelihood opportunities and increased participation of women and youth in the local economy.
