Who We Are

Who We Are

TARUDEREO works alongside rural communities and local stakeholders to address development challenges and create sustainable opportunities for vulnerable and underserved populations. Our approach is based on community participation, local knowledge, partnerships, accountability and sustainable solutions.

Our Mission

Our Mission

To contribute to sustainable rural development by empowering communities, protecting the environment, improving health and education, strengthening agriculture and expanding economic opportunities.

Our Vision

Our Vision

A healthy, educated, economically empowered and environmentally sustainable rural society where communities can achieve a better quality of life.

Our Story

Registered Since 2002, Re-registered in 2023

Based in Mission Street, Murubona Ward, Kasulu District, Kigoma Region, TARUDEREO is registered by the Ministry of Community Development, Gender, Women and Special Groups under the NGO Act No. 24 (Certificate No. ooNGO/R/5461, issued 29 September 2023).
